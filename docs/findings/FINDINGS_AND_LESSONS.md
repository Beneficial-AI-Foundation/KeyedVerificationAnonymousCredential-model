# Findings and lessons from the repository record

The KVAC team (Christiano Braga, Jin Xing Lim, Semar Augusto, Marco Stronati, Oana Ciobotaru, Callum Bannister)

31 October 2026

The project formalizes in Lean 4 the keyed-verification anonymous credentials of Orrù's paper, *Revisiting Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials* (IACR ePrint 2024/1552), with their security proofs. This report merges the two files in which the project recorded its departures from the paper so far. The errata, [docs/presentations/rolf-status/errata.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/presentations/rolf-status/errata.md), hold the discrepancies between the printed text and what the paper's own arguments require. The design record, [docs/DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md), holds the decisions taken where the paper says less than a formalization needs. Both files stay in the repository with their full text, frozen, and receive no new items. This report is the record from now on, in the format of the team's reports of October 2026.

**How to add an item.** A new finding about the paper goes at the end of the table of its category and takes the next label of that table, so that labels never change once given and every reference to an item stays valid, one row with that label, its source, the source section, what is wrong in one sentence, the Lean object that carries the correction and its origin. Its full account, the printed text, why it is wrong, the proposed correction and where the formalization records it, lives at its source, an errata entry, a design record entry or an issue, which the Source column links. The report does not repeat it. A new design decision goes into Part A6 as one bullet under the layer of the paper it concerns, or under the item of Part A it answers, with the decision, the rejected alternative and the fidelity argument in full, and the date. A lesson that the record supports goes to Part B with the items or decisions it rests on. A lesson on engineering goes to Part C and a lesson on the use of AI assistants to Part D. The status list and the "What we did" paragraphs are revised when a statement or a proof lands.

## Part A. Findings about the paper

The conventions of Part A follow.

- **The paper.** Orrù, *Revisiting Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials*, IACR ePrint 2024/1552, cited as O24, in its version of 27 March 2026 unless a version is named.
- **Labels.** The first column of the tables labels the items by table, A1.1 to A1.6 in A1 and so on. The prose refers to items by that label.
- **Source.** The second column gives the source of the row, linked to its text. It is the number of the same item in the frozen [errata.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/presentations/rolf-status/errata.md), which the docstrings and the design record cite, the heading of a design record entry, or the candidate number on [#217](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/217). Every item has its full account at its source, the printed text, the reason and the correction, and the report does not repeat it.
- **DA.** The abbreviation stands for the design record, [docs/DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md). A reference of the form "DA *Conditioned η masks*" points at the entry of that file whose heading is *Conditioned η masks*, where the decision, the rejected alternative and the fidelity argument are written in full.
- **Implementation.** Items A3.2, A3.3 and A3.6 change an algorithm of the scheme, so an implementation has to follow them. Every other item lives only in the proof of a security theorem.
- **Branch.** The corrected statements of items A4.2 and A4.3 are on the branch of [#184](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/184), every other one on `main`.
- **Proof status.** A corrected statement is stated, not proved. Every security statement of the repository depends on `sorry`. The Lean statement of Lemma 5.4 has a full proof over two admitted lemmas, the game equality and the 3/p bound. Every other security theorem has `sorry` as its proof. The reductions of Lemmas 5.4 and 5.5 are implemented definitions. The three reductions of `omufBound` are definitions with `sorry` bodies until the proofs of Claims 5.12 to 5.14 build them.

Items A1.1 to A1.4, A2.7, A3.1 and A4.1 to A4.3 change a printed statement, the others correct a proof step or a sampler. The proofs of the corrected statements are open, so each correction is a stated target, not an established result, until its proof closes.

**What we did.** Every formal statement is an inequality on named reductions.

- Item A1.1. The Lemma 5.4 bound is Adv^3-dl(B₁) + 3/p, where the paper prints Adv^3-dl + Adv^dl + 1/p. The 3/p is the generic Schwartz–Zippel bound for a polynomial of degree 3. The design record leaves open whether a structural argument recovers the printed 1/p, which the paper does not derive (DA *Schwartz–Zippel bound 3/p, not the paper's 1/p*).
- Item A1.2. The same bound has no Adv^dl term.
- Item A1.3. The Lemma 5.5 bound is Adv^3-dl(B₁) + Adv^gap-dl(B_gap) + 5/p, where the paper prints Adv^3-dl + Adv^dl + 3/p. The gap-DL term is the collision case of Claim 5.6. The 5/p is the 3/p of Lemma 5.4 plus 1/p for the event Σᵢ Δᵢbᵢ = 0 of Claim 5.6 plus 1/p for the event x₁ = 0 of Claim 5.7. The bound is on the algebraic game with Help. Theorem 5.1 for that game is [PR 256](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/256).
- Item A1.4. `omufBound` and `omufBoundN1` state the printed constants. Whether they hold is decided by the proofs of Claims 5.12 to 5.14, as item A1.1 was for Lemma 5.4. The item is recorded as an obligation on the Claims.
- Item A1.5. Both lemma statements, `agm_ufcmva_le_n1_explicit` and `agm_ufcmva_le_explicit`, are inequalities.
- Item A1.6. No code carries the two sentences. The docstrings of the gap-DL reduction and of the Lemma 5.5 statement state the two events with probability at most 1/p.

### A6. Things the paper leaves implicit that the formalization had to decide

Part A6 lists the design decisions of the formalization, the places where the paper says less than a machine needs. Each entry states the decision, the rejected alternative and the fidelity argument. The entries follow the layers of the paper, from the proof systems of §3.3 to the anonymous token of §5.6. A decision that corrects an item of the tables above is cited in that item's row, not repeated here. Two open questions close the part.

A6.1 **The proof-system layer, §3.3.**

- **Extractor and simulator exhibited existentially.** Both are exhibited in the security statements, not fields of the scheme, as in §3.3 and the standard definitions.
- **Monadic verifier.** `verify` returns `M Bool`, since the paper writes 0/1 ← ZKP.V with a sampling arrow and a Fiat–Shamir verifier recomputes its challenge through the random oracle.
- **Proveᵦ guard on the oracle side.** The Proveᵦ oracle checks (x, w) ∈ R and answers `none` on a non-witnessed query, identically in both worlds. The entry rejects a type-level guard on the query, since the check belongs to the oracle and a type restriction narrows the game's interface.
- **Relation decidability scoped to the game.** A `Decidable` instance is data, so the game that runs the guard takes it as an argument, and `relation` stays a `Prop`.
- **White-box knowledge-soundness extractor.** The extractor takes the adversary value and the run's observables, the output, the final oracle table and, for simulation extractability, the simulation log, as §3.3 has Ext take "the random coins and the code" of A. Rewinding is out of scope, the §9 instantiation being straight-line. Whether an ordered query log is needed is open.
- **Carrier `OracleComp (ZKRO H)` for setup, prove and verify.** One carrier, so `verify` can read the oracle the proof was built against. That `verify` draws no coins of its own is deferred to a lemma ([#101](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/101)), two bespoke structures that fix it in the type being rejected for their cost at every use site.
- **Structured hash domains.** `HashSpec.Dom` is an arbitrary type, a stronger model than bitstrings, with the injective encoding and the domain separation as obligations of a concrete instantiation.

A6.2 **The framework, §4.**

- **Exact-attribute predicate as a syntax field.** `KVACSyntax` carries φ_m⃗ as `exactPred` with its semantic law `holds_exactPred`, since Figure 8's NewUsr runs issuance under it, while `PredicateFamily` holds the Definition 4.1 content. The design record entry *Exact-attribute predicate as a family field* predates the move of the field to `KVACSyntax` on 12 August. The rest of the partial-disclosure family is [#104](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/104).
- **Correctness requires issuance to succeed before presentation runs.** The experiment asks for the event σ ≠ ⊥ ∧ b = 1, since the paper defines presentation on credentials only.
- **No obligation theorem for `ExtractablePoly`.** The theorem asserting extractability for every scheme without premises was false, and [#177](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/177) removed it. The asymptotic clause of Theorem 5.10 is [#148](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/148).
- **Anonymity game at fixed setup, negligibility over instance families.** The games take the crs, the keys, the attributes, the predicate and the initial table as parameters, as Definition 4.4 quantifies pointwise, since a game that samples setup bounds an average and an average does not bound every crs.
- **The issuer adversary runs before and after the user's request**, with random-oracle access in both runs, so that a simulator that reprograms the oracle at its first move is detectable.
- **Two adversary structures, not one pair.** A and D are separate, as the statistical and everlasting variants of Definition 4.4 constrain them separately.
- **The anonymity simulator runs in the random-oracle state monad**, so it may reprogram the table, the power §3.3 grants a simulator.
- **The Present oracle answers under the queried predicate** φ′, the printed φ being read as a typo.
- **The extractor of Definition 4.5 takes the random-oracle table** as of the call and runs in `ProbComp`, since the proof of Theorem 5.10 builds it from the proof systems' extractors, which read the trace.

A6.3 **The MAC and its reductions, §5.3.**

- **Conditioned sign masks** and **conditioned η masks.** The reduction's masks are sampled conditioned on Uⱼ ≠ 0 and on H ≠ 0. The real game draws both from the nonzero elements, so the game equalities of Lemma 5.4 are false otherwise.
- **Help in the algebraic MAC game from the start.** The algebraic UF-CMVA game carries the Help oracle since [PR 73](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/73), as the docstring of [KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AlgebraicMAC.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AlgebraicMAC.lean) and the blueprint node `agm_model` record, since the paper proves Theorem 5.1 with Help for the later proofs (p. 35). From Jin's report.
- **Nonzero tag base U in the base MAC, where Figure 9 prints 𝔾.** μCMZ.M samples U from the nonzero elements with `uniformNonzero` (`mac`, [Construction.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/Construction.lean)), and a proof that needs U ≠ 0 obtains it from `mem_support_uniformNonzero`. The rejected alternative is Figure 9's U ←$ 𝔾 (p. 34), under which U = 0 with probability 1/p. Eq. (1) (p. 11) draws U from the nonzero elements. Definition 3.1 (p. 25) requires every honest MAC to verify, which the predicate `Correct` formalizes and `μCMZBaseMAC_correct` proves under this sampler. The simulator of Claim 5.6 draws its signing scalar from the nonzero scalars for the same reason, `gapSignScalarSample` in [GapDLReduction.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AGMReduction/GapDLReduction.lean). The one entry of this part that corrects an error of the paper, item A3.6, with no design record entry yet. Proposed on [#217](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/217), 3 October, accepted 30 October.
- **Claim 5.7, the scalars of the reduction to n = 1.** The reduction samples all n scalars rᵢ with Xᵢ = rᵢX₁ uniformly, `nTo1Adversary`, where the paper fixes z₁ = 1 and samples z₂, …, zₙ (pp. 39–40), at the same 1/p and with no distinguished index.
- **`WellBehaved` and the translation to the plain game.** *Superseded.* The first encoding of the algebraic group model answered a query with an inconsistent representation by false. It needed the hypothesis `WellBehaved` at every key, which also rejected every Help query. Issue [#235](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/235) replaces it by an adversary type that submits coefficients only, with a forget map into the game with Help and a transfer lemma without side condition. PRs [246](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/246) and [247](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/247), merged 30 October, delete the bridge and restrict the MAC game, recorded in the entry *Algebraic adversaries submit coefficients only* of the [design record](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md?plain=1#L663). PRs [256](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/256) and [267](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/267), open, state Theorem 5.1 for the game with Help and prove the transfer.
- **Theorem 5.10 at the honest UF-CMVA game with Help.** The unforgeability term of Theorem 5.10 is the advantage in the honest Figure 5 game with the oracle Help answered by the challenger, since the proof of Theorem 5.10 uses Help's answer and the paper ties Help to no model ([PR 243](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/243)).

A6.4 **Anonymity of μCMZ, §5.4.**

- **Theorem 5.8 at a fixed crs.** The §3.3 advantages in the bound are taken on views that fix the crs at the H of the anonymity game, since a pointwise advantage is not bounded by sampled ones in general.
- **The anonymity simulator takes a trace extractor**, a function of the crs, the statement, the proof and the table. Definition 4.4 puts ∃Sim before ∀A, so the simulator cannot depend on the adversary a `KSNDExtractor` takes. The §9 extractor also reads the algebraic representations, which the instantiation of [#3](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/3) must carry in the proof or in the table.

A6.5 **One-more unforgeability and the anonymous token, §5.6 and §5.2.**

- **Exact-length representations in the AGM OMUF game.** The game accepts a representation only with one coefficient per issued pair, so it evaluates the same under any later transcript. A simplification, not a soundness requirement, with three alternatives recorded.
- **Every Sign query counts toward q.** The Sign oracle of `OMUFGame` logs each request whether or not the server rejects it, since Figure 6 increments q before the response. The budget q is a hypothesis on the adversary, not a field of its type ([KVAC/Preliminaries/AnonymousTokens/Security.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Preliminaries/AnonymousTokens/Security.lean), [PR 143](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/143)). From Jin's report.
- **Restricted algebraic one-more forger.** *In open PRs.* The game of [#181](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/181) checks the representation of each query for consistency with the transcript, with the bridge to the plain Figure 6 game deferred as the one-more counterpart of the `WellBehaved` bridge ([#81](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/81)). Issue [#235](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/235), parts 5 to 7, replaces the consistency check by a forger that submits coefficients only, normalized to the issued pairs, with a forget map into the plain game and a transfer lemma without side condition. Theorem 5.11 then bounds the plain Figure 6 advantage of each algebraic forger, the advantage the paper states (pp. 27, 43). Part 5 is [PR 268](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/268), opened on 29 October. Parts 6 and 7 have no PR yet. From Semar's report.
- **Theorem 5.11 stated before its reductions exist.** The reductions are definitions with `sorry` bodies at the whole function type, named in the bound. Existential reductions are vacuous at a fixed group. A uniform ε on every discrete-log adversary is false or trivial. A `sorry` under the binders makes f A₁ = f A₂ hold by `rfl`. Claims 5.12 and 5.13 each name their own reduction, the theorem's being their mixture.
- **One-more forgers have a private coin oracle.** The forger the lifting lemma builds samples a random-oracle cache and simulator runs, which a coinless type cannot express ([PR 232](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/232)).
- **Two carriers for one-more unforgeability.** The core and Figure 6 stay at `ProbComp`. The scheme with π_is runs at the oracle carrier with its own game, through one `runRO`. One experiment `omufExp` is generic in the carrier, with a bridge equating the lifted advantage with the `ProbComp` one ([#183](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/183)).
- **Theorem 5.3 takes its clauses from Theorems 5.8 and 5.11.** *On the branch.* Three clauses. Correctness. Definition 4.4 anonymity on the token as a credential, with the Theorem 5.8 simulator minus its π_iu part, without the Remark 5.9 hypotheses. One-more unforgeability with the Theorem 5.11 bound plus the π_is term. The three `omufBound` reductions take the π_is simulator, with the Sign budget a hypothesis counted by `OMUFQueryRO.isSign`. The node of the printed theorem names no Lean declarations, citing the milestone node of the corrected statement per the honesty rule of [docs/BLUEPRINT_MAINTENANCE.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/BLUEPRINT_MAINTENANCE.md). The clause on the branch is on the plain game of Figure 6 over every adversary, which claims more than the paper proves. The decision of 28 October is to state it in the algebraic group model as printed, which needs a game in that model for the scheme with π_is, of the size of [#181](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/181), so [#184](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/184) is postponed.

A6.6 **Decisions not yet taken.** Two open questions, the first an open alternative of the design record, the second a proposed entry on #240.

- **The crs and the group** ([#148](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/148)). The instance fixes the group and the generator as type parameters, so every theorem is an inequality at one group, with no λ. The λ-indexed family is the committed step, and until it exists the asymptotic predicates are not provable for μCMZ.
- **Adversary classes and the paper's p.p.t. class** ([#240](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/240)). Every adversary is an `OracleComp` computation with no running time. Under "for all adversaries" the wider class strengthens a theorem, under "there exists a simulator" it weakens a definition. The one-more forgers contain the paper's class since [PR 232](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/232). The UF-CMVA forgers, the extractors and the efficiency parameters are classified only in the comments of the issue, the forgers as incomparable, with no entry in the design record. O24 bounds neither the simulator of Definition 4.4 nor the extractor of Definition 4.5, in the definitions or in §3.3, while the two reference papers read for [#240](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/240) fix the zero-knowledge simulator as p.p.t. and the knowledge-soundness extractor as expected polynomial time. The bound the formalization adopts is its own choice, decided by what the proofs of Theorems 5.8 and 5.10 establish for the simulator and the extractor they build (Oana's report).

## Part B. Lessons about formalizing the paper

### B1. Bounds and statements

1. **A printed term without a reduction is unexplained, and a missing one is a gap.** The printed Adv^dl of Lemma 5.4 (item A1.2) and the missing gap-DL term of Theorem 5.1 (item A1.3) appeared when the statement had to name a reduction for each summand. Drop a term only once the bound without it has a proof, since a nonnegative summand never makes an upper bound false. A constant is derived the same way. The 3/p of item A1.1 and the missing Verify term of item A1.4 appeared when a statement had to say which event has which probability. In the code, `agm_ufcmva_le_n1_explicit` ([PR 155](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/155)) and `agm_ufcmva_le_explicit` ([PR 209](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/209)) name their reductions.
2. **An unrestricted existential reduction at a fixed finite group proves nothing.** A perfect discrete-log solver exists on a finite group, so "there exists B" without a resource bound is vacuous, and "every adversary has advantage at most ε" is false or trivial. The unforgeability statements name their reductions, while the anonymity statement still quantifies its three reductions existentially (DA *Theorem 5.11 stated before its reductions exist*). Naming a reduction fixes which algorithm the bound is about and leaves its efficiency to the asymptotic layer. The named reductions are `microCMZ3DLReduction` ([AGMReduction/Core.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AGMReduction/Core.lean), [PR 88](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/88)), `gapDlReduction` ([PR 208](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/208)) and the three of `omufBound` ([OneMoreUnforgeability/Statements.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/OneMoreUnforgeability/Statements.lean), [PR 190](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/190) and [PR 198](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/198)). The existential form stands in `μCMZ_anonymity` ([Anonymity.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/Anonymity.lean), [PR 228](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/228)).
3. **Summary theorems drift from the theorems the paper proves.** Theorems 1, 5.2 and 5.3 are overview copies of §5 results, stated first and proved later (p. 35). Items A4.2 and A4.3 are copies that no revision updated, item A4.1 a copy that changed a term. Each summary is read on the scheme it names, which is how item A4.2 charges a proof the token never sends. Derive the overview statement from the theorems the paper proves, with the version history in hand. The corrected statement is `μCMZAT_anonymous_token`, [AnonymousToken.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/microcmz-theorem-5-3/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AnonymousToken.lean) on the branch of [#184](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/184).
4. **A theorem without premises about every scheme is false.** The theorem `extractablePoly_obligation` of [PR 126](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/126) asserted `ExtractablePoly` for every scheme and every efficiency predicate, with no hypothesis on the scheme. A scheme whose server accepts every presentation, under the constantly true efficiency predicate, refutes it, so [#177](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/177) removed it on 12 October ([b3e1f2d](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/commit/b3e1f2d)). The premises its docstring elided, scheme correctness and knowledge-sound proof systems, are ones an abstract scheme cannot name, so a statement that holds for a class of schemes names the class or goes. The asymptotic clause of Theorem 5.10 waits for the λ-indexed group family of [#148](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/148) (DA *No obligation theorem for `ExtractablePoly`*). Lesson 4 of Part C of Christiano's report.
5. **The formalization finds typos by refusing to couple, not by rereading.** None of items A2.1 to A2.6 and A2.8 changes a result, and each stopped a proof until it was found. Item A2.7 changes which proof system a term of the Theorem 5.8 bound names. Eqs. 13 and 14 surfaced when the coupling between the reduction and the real game would not close with the printed coefficients, in `microCMZ3DLReduction` and `reductionSignStep` ([PR 88](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/88)). A reader who knows what a coefficient has to be reads that value into the equation and never notices the printed one. A check that uses the printed value as written finds it. Items A2.9 to A2.11 came from a review or from a written proof before any Lean proof reached them. The A2 lesson of Jin's report, lesson C1 of Semar's report.

### B2. Schemes, adversaries and quantifiers

6. **Ask "what if it is zero" of every sampled or received value**, in the scheme and in the reduction. It gave items A3.2, A3.3 and A3.4 on the scheme side, where the answer decides correctness (item A3.2), a security theorem (item A3.3) or a simulation step (item A3.4). On the reduction side it gave the two conditioned-mask entries, where a game equality is false until the reduction excludes the same zero the game excludes. In the code, `uniformUnits` ([PR 146](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/146)), `setup` with `uniformNonzero` ([PR 216](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/216)), `reductionMaskSample` ([SignMask.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/SignMask.lean), [PR 48](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/48)) and the η masks of [PR 229](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/229). Decide the sampling domains before the proofs. Each later change, the nonzero masks and the nonzero H, cost a lemma for every proof that read the old distribution (lesson 9 of Jin's report).
7. **One proof may analyze a smaller scheme than the theorem names.** Figure 9 and Theorem 5.3 name the token with π_is, while the proof of Theorem 5.11 analyzes the core without it (item A3.1). The way out is to state the smaller scheme under its own name with the printed bound, then prove the difference in a separate lemma, not yet defined ([#205](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/205)). In the code, `μCMZATCoreSyntax` ([ATVariant.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/ATVariant.lean), [PR 147](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/147)) and `μCMZATSyntax` ([ATVariantPiIs.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/ATVariantPiIs.lean), [PR 238](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/238)).
8. **A hypothesis on the adversary that stands in for a model restriction belongs in the adversary's type.** The hypothesis `WellBehaved` was semantic, quantified over every key, and stood in for "the adversary is algebraic". Nothing proved it was the paper's notion, and it excluded the Help oracle that a downstream proof needs. The paper proves the stronger claim one paragraph after Theorem 5.1, and the proof of Theorem 5.10 uses it. The statement to formalize comes from the proof that follows the theorem and from the proofs that use it, not from the boxed text alone. The [#235](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/235) stack makes a non-algebraic adversary unrepresentable, so that the transfer lemma has no side condition. Whether that type is the paper's class is then a theorem to prove. A hypothesis that is the paper's own, such as the Sign budget q, stays a hypothesis. The consistency check was in `agmOracleImpl` and in the win condition of `AGM_UF_CMVAGame` ([AlgebraicMAC.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/AlgebraicMAC.lean), [PR 73](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/73)). Its removal is PRs [246](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/246) and [247](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/247), merged 30 October.
9. **The adversary's type is part of the theorem.** Coins ([PR 232](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/232)), the Help oracle ([PR 243](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/243)) and the encoding of the algebraic group model each changed which forgers a bound covers. Each decision came when a downstream proof needed a forger the type could not express. In the code, `OMUFAdversary` over `unifSpec` ([Preliminaries/AnonymousTokens/Security.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Preliminaries/AnonymousTokens/Security.lean), [PR 232](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/232)), `UF_CMVAHelpGame` ([UFCMVAHelp.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/UFCMVAHelp.lean), [PR 243](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/243)) and `AGMUFAdversary` ([PR 73](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/73)).
10. **The order of quantifiers decides what a simulator may see.** Placing ∃Sim before ∀A rules out a simulator that reads the adversary, which motivates a trace extractor, whose existence for the compiled proof systems is still open (DA *The anonymity simulator takes a trace extractor*). An oracle adversary that queries at any time forces two runs of the issuer (DA *The issuer adversary runs before and after the user's request*). In the code, `μCMZAnonSimulator` takes `extIs` through `risExtToTrace` ([Anonymity.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Schemes/MicroCMZ/Anonymity.lean), [PR 228](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/228)). The structure `AnonIssuer` has two moves ([Framework/Anonymity.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Framework/Anonymity.lean), [PR 186](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/186)).
11. **Pointwise and sampled quantifiers need a bridge.** Definition 4.4 quantifies over every crs while the §3.3 games sample it, and an average does not bound every point (DA *Anonymity game at fixed setup*, *Theorem 5.8 at a fixed crs*). The fixed group is the same question one level up ([#148](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/148)). In the code, `anonGameRun` takes the crs ([Framework/Anonymity.lean](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/KVAC/Framework/Anonymity.lean), [PR 186](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/186)). The theorem `μCMZ_anonymity` reads the advantages at that crs ([PR 228](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/228)).

### B3. Fidelity and the record

12. **A fidelity claim quotes the paper. Where the paper is silent, the entry marks the decision as the formalization's own.** Every entry of the design record cites the definition, figure, equation or section it rests on, with the page where it quotes the text, or says that the paper leaves the point open ([#240](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/240)). Public prose, docstrings and blueprint nodes, cites the paper's definitions, equations and pages, never the plan. Issue numbers stay in PR bodies (lesson 4 of Jin's report).

The theorem keeps its statement instead of fitting the game at hand (DA *Theorem 5.3 takes its clauses from Theorems 5.8 and 5.11*, [#184](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/184)). The game to build is the algebraic one-more game for the scheme with π_is, not yet in the code. The statement on the branch is `μCMZAT_anonymous_token`.
14. **Every departure gets an entry with a date and an owner, then goes upstream early.** Item A3.1 went to Rolfe on 20 August with a proposed correction. When the same question returned two months later, the π_is variant and the game carrier, the entry answered it. This report is the deliverable of every review, since the errata and the design record are frozen. A PR that departs from the paper without an entry goes back (lesson 3 of Jin's report).
15. **A risk written down is still a risk.** Issue [#81](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/81), opened on 22 July, names the choice between checking the representations of the queries and restricting the adversary type as a semantic change still to decide. The track built four PRs on the consistency check before [#235](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/235) decided the question on 13 October. From Semar's report. The consistency check came with [PR 73](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/73). Its removal is PRs [246](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/246) and [247](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/247), merged 30 October.
16. **Write the paper's proof out in full before formalizing it.** The written proofs of Lemma 5.5 with Claims 5.6 and 5.7 ([docs/CRYPTOGRAPHIC_PROOFS_MICROCMZ_LEMMA55.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/CRYPTOGRAPHIC_PROOFS_MICROCMZ_LEMMA55.md), [PR 212](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/212)) yielded seven findings, items A1.5, A1.6, A2.10, A2.11, A3.6, A5.1 and A5.2. A Lean statement fixes what a theorem says. The written proof checks every step. ## Part C. Lessons about engineering and process

These lessons rest on the reports of Jin, Oana and Semar, lessons 6 and 7 also on the repository record. This part reproduces them with their authors, merging the pairs that describe one phenomenon.

### C1. Lean and VCV-io

1. **Two programs that compute the same distribution are not equal.** `OracleComp` is a free monad, so equality of programs is syntactic. Correctness is a statement about the output distribution or its support, never about the programs. Lesson 6 of Jin's report.
2. **Instance search costs more days than mathematics.** A `SubtractionCommMonoid` diamond through the `AddCommGroup` projection of `PrimeOrderGroup` made a cold build hang until one `attribute [instance 50]` brought it to seconds. A discrete-log helper needed the `irreducible` attribute, since unfolding it in a polynomial-ring context sent typeclass search into a loop. Importing a library umbrella in place of the two modules needed turned a two-minute CI into a six-hour hang. Import narrowly, mark irreducible the definitions no proof unfolds, write an explicit instance where search loops. Lesson 7 of Jin's report, lesson C4 of Semar's report.
3. **A definitional equality that needs an unfolding is invisible to `rw` and `simp`.** Those tactics match at reducible transparency, which unfolds only the constants tagged `@[reducible]`, with instances added for `rw`, while the definitional equality of term mode unfolds every constant except those tagged `@[irreducible]`. A lift inserted by a coercion and one written by hand elaborate with different instance terms, so `rw` and `simp` fail on goals that print identically. After the v4.30 bump `simp` stalled on binds whose type arguments differ only up to unfolding. Term-mode `bind_assoc`, `map_bind` and `bind_congr` close them, and the bridge goes into one lemma. Lesson 8 of Jin's report, lesson C5 of Semar's report.

### C2. Builds and version control

4. **A warm editor is not a build.** A lemma with the discrete log of a subtype coercion in a kernel-checked term looked proved in the editor and hung a cold `lake build` for hours, since `maxHeartbeats` does not bound the kernel. "Proved" means a cold build plus `#print axioms`. Lesson C3 of Semar's report.
5. **Git drops content silently.** After a squash merge every child branch of a stack conflicts, and `git rebase --onto main <old parent tip>` after each merge, one PR at a time, keeps the stack moving. A merge dropped a block of about 689 lines with no conflict, visible only as cascading unknown identifiers. The command `git rebase --update-refs` moved backup branches that sat inside the rebased range. Record the tip SHAs before every rebase and read `git range-diff` after. Lesson 5 of Jin's report, lesson C6 of Semar's report.

### C3. The blueprint as status board

6. **Anchor a milestone node only when all its declarations exist.** Statements go on `main` with `sorry` before any proof, each sub-lemma a blueprint node, so that reviews of a statement catch the modelling errors before anyone spends proof effort. The rule that makes it safe is in [docs/BLUEPRINT_MAINTENANCE.md](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/docs/BLUEPRINT_MAINTENANCE.md), never half-anchor a milestone. Lesson 1 of Jin's report.
7. **One tracker, computed from the code.** The team stopped the progress table generated from hand-maintained TOML sources. Its workflow runs on manual dispatch only, and the table was last regenerated on 14 August. The blueprint, with the CI check that exactly one node anchors each public declaration ([scripts/blueprint_coverage_check.py](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/blob/main/scripts/blueprint_coverage_check.py)), is the only status board, since hand-maintained tables drifted within weeks. Lesson 2 of Jin's report.

## Part D. Lessons about the use of AI assistants

These lessons concern the machine reviewers and the coding assistants the team used. Lessons 1 to 3 come from the reports of Jin, Oana and Semar, lessons 4 and 5 from Christiano's report. None appears in another part.

### D1. Machine reviewers and agents

1. **Machine reviewers need checking at the source.** The track ran two reviewers blind to each other. Codex was strong on counterexamples and reuse. Kimi inflated severity and once read "no sorry in the body" as "proved". The track rejected about a third of their claims. A reviewer's "this does not compile" compiled on v4.30, and a claimed `IsUniformSpec (ZKRO H)` instance did not exist. Test each claim on the toolchain, run the axiom audit after a cold build, read every diff before a commit, never let an agent push. Lesson 11 of Jin's report, lesson C8 of Semar's report.
2. **An assistant's draft is a lead, not a finding.** Drafts for PRs [213](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/213) and [216](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/216) contained an invented lemma name, a stale file count and a wrong diff count. Only the subset checked at the source went out. The rules since are agents in ask mode, every command explained before approval, every quoted line reread in the file, verified and inferred kept apart. Lesson 4 of Oana's report.
3. **Parallel reviews with sub-agents need two rules.** Each agent has its own worktree and writes its final file before handing back. The coordinator re-verifies the main claims. One day's work reviewed six PRs this way, with the coordinator restarting agents that did not resume on their own. Lesson 12 of Jin's report.

### D2. Terminology and sources of truth

4. **The use of AI across the team has to be consistent.** Terms with no basis in the paper or in the literature, "constant audit", "collapse" and "direction" for the scalars of the reduction to n = 1, entered docstrings and blueprint nodes through one assistant, and other assistants copied them from file to file. The rule that bans coined terms in new text extends to the terms already in the code base. The team replaces them with no exception for the terms already in use. The first replacements went in with [PR 241](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/241) on 29 October, which closed [#239](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/issues/239). The three terms above are not in its table and stay in the code base, "collapse" in the names `AttributeCollapse.lean`, `collapseRepr` and `collapseOracleImpl`. Christiano's report.
5. **The code base is not a source of truth for an assistant.** The paper and the written proofs are, and the code base reflects them. When a docstring or a design entry becomes the reference an assistant reads, an error propagates. The design record's reading of Claim 5.7, that the paper's reduction owes no translation of representations, went from the design record into the module docstring and into the first draft of this report, although the paper states Lemma 5.4 in the algebraic group model (p. 36) and the written proof of Lemma 5.5 ([PR 212](https://github.com/Beneficial-AI-Foundation/KeyedVerificationAnonymousCredential-model/pull/212)) had already said so. Item A5.2 is the corrected record. Christiano's report.
