01The main theorems

O24 states its core theorems up front (§5.2) and proves them later in the section: 5.1 (the MAC), 5.2 (the credential) and 5.3 (the anonymous token). Theorem 5.2 is an umbrella — its two displayed bounds are verbatim Theorems 5.8 (anonymity) and 5.10 (extractability), so those are grouped beneath it, with 5.1 supplying the ufcmva leaf inside 5.10. Theorem 5.3 is likewise a bundle — the 5.8 simulator argument on the token variant plus one-more unforgeability, proved as Theorem 5.11. Follow a theorem's number for its dependency map.

02Build phasesall three core theorems

The order in which the theorems become provable. Foundations and Theorem 5.1 are Semar's algebraic-MAC track; the framework games, the μCMZ instance, and the credential theorems (5.8, 5.10, 5.2) are the credential track; the anonymous-token theorems (5.11, 5.3) are Track CMZ-OMUF and run on a separate path. These are engineering steps — the maps below show which node each phase produces.

  1. Foundations on main

    NIZKP §3.3 (zero-knowledge #47, knowledge-soundness / SE #54), the algebraic-MAC and μCMZ MAC with the AGM UF-CMVA(+Help) game, and the Schnorr relations — all merged.

  2. Theorem 5.1 — UF-CMVA in flight

    The AGM reduction — Lemma 5.4 (n=1) lifted by Lemma 5.5 (n=poly). The reduction core (#88) is now merged on main. The coupling + Schwartz–Zippel PR (#129) is the only one still open; sign-coupling and the remaining auxiliary lemmas are tracked as issues #106–#116 and are not yet merged. Then, for 5.10 to cite, the sorry-able AGM bound agm_ufcmva_le — plus a decision on the AGM ↔ standard UF-CMVA gap (below). Semar's track.

  3. Framework games — Def 4.5 & Def 4.4 partial

    The extraction game (Def 4.5, the #119→#126 stack, merging) for 5.10, and the anonymity game (Def 4.4, Anonymity.lean, not written) for 5.8 — abstract games over any KVACSyntax.

  4. μCMZ as a KVAC instance — Credential.lean not written

    Instantiate KVACSyntax (OracleComp (ZKRO H)) for μCMZ — setup/keygen from the base MAC, issue*/present* from the Schnorr sub-proofs through an abstract ZKP. The single shared unblocker: without it, neither 5.8 nor 5.10 can be stated.

  5. Credential theorems — 5.10, 5.8, then 5.2 not written

    State each over its hypotheses and prove its reduction — three game hops for extractability (5.10), simulation for anonymity (5.8). Theorem 5.2 is then their conjunction.

  6. Anonymous-token track — 5.11, then 5.3 not written

    The anonymous-token syntax and OMUF game (§3.4, Fig 6), the μCMZAT core instance (Fig 9 minus πiu), and the AGM analysis of Theorem 5.11 (Eq 17–23, Claims 5.12–5.14). Runs in parallel to phases 3–5: the OMUF half needs neither the μCMZ KVAC instance nor Theorem 5.1. Theorem 5.3's anonymity half waits on 5.8. Track CMZ-OMUF, issue #12.

Critical path. The single unblocker is the μCMZ KVAC instance (Credential.lean, step 4 above) — until it exists, neither 5.8 nor 5.10 can even be stated. Once it lands, both credential reductions build on pieces already merged: the μCMZ MAC and the UF-CMVA games are on main. Extractability (5.10) closes its ufcmva leaf through Theorem 5.1, which needs two still-missing pieces on Semar's side — the AGM → UF-CMVA bridge (standard vs AGM-instrumented game) and the sorry-able AGM bound agm_ufcmva_le, both in no PR — but not the full AGM reduction proof (#129 + issues #106–#116; core #88 already merged), which stays sorry'd behind the bound. Anonymity (5.8) additionally needs the Def 4.4 anonymity game, still unwritten. Two blockers sit underneath, and they are model mismatches, not mere postponements: the ProbComp vs OracleComp (ZKRO H) carrier bridge (issue #118) — the concrete instance may not even inhabit the framework type 5.2 uses — and the abstract isPPT predicate, which carries no closure and could be instantiated vacuously. Until both are settled, the honest deliverable is a pointwise, finite-parameter advantage inequality, not asymptotic "μCMZ is secure". The anonymous-token theorem (5.3) sits on a separate path: its OMUF half (5.11) needs neither the μCMZ KVAC instance nor Theorem 5.1 — only its anonymity half waits on 5.8.

technique · algebraic group model

Represent the adversary's group elements as generator combinations and reduce a forgery to a non-zero degree-≤3 polynomial identity in the exponent, bounded by Schwartz–Zippel (the 3/p root bound) over the 3-DL and DL assumptions. The n=1 case is Lemma 5.4 (the reduction core #88 is merged; coupling + Schwartz–Zippel is the open PR #129; sign-coupling and the auxiliary lemmas are issues #106–#116, not yet merged); Lemma 5.5 lifts to n=poly. This bounds the AGM-instrumented game AGM_UF_CMVA, where the adversary must supply algebraic representations. Connecting it to the standard UF_CMVA is a modeling decision, not a free lemma: an unrestricted adversary need not supply representations, so 5.10 must either be stated over generic UF_CMVA or restricted to algebraic credential adversaries (Semar's flag; sharpened by the cross-model review).

technique · simulation

Simulate the issuance and presentation proofs (zero-knowledge), so the adversary's view becomes independent of the honest secret attributes — two zk terms. The simulator recovers the secret x0 by running the issuance extractor on πis (hybrid H3), which pays the third term: the knowledge-soundness advantage ksnd_is, per the paper's Theorem 5.8 statement (p. 40) — so the KS extractor (#54) appears in the bound itself, not just the proof. Blocked on the anonymity game (Def 4.4, unwritten) and the μCMZ instance.

technique · three game hops

The extraction game's winning cases split across the four bound terms — one hybrid step (zk_is) plus three terminal events — so the mapping is not a clean one-to-one. The partition must be proved to cover every win (aborts, replays, malformed proofs, extractor failures) with a union bound:

  1. Replace real proofs with simulated ones — drift bounded by zk_is.
  2. Extract witnesses via the ZKP extractors — the Issue abort and a non-opening presentation bounded by ksnd_iu / ksnd_p. Because extraction happens after simulated proofs are exposed, this likely needs simulation-extractability (seGame, #54), not plain knowledge-soundness.
  3. A verifying presentation whose extracted attributes were never issued is a forgery — bounded by ufcmva (Theorem 5.1).

The reduction machinery is buildable on main today — the μCMZ MAC instance and the UF-CMVA games are merged. Closing the ufcmva leaf then needs two things on the 5.1 side: a decision on the AGM ↔ standard UF-CMVA gap (restrict 5.10 to algebraic adversaries, or state it over generic UF_CMVA — a soundness question, not a free lemma), and the sorry-able AGM bound agm_ufcmva_le. Neither waits on the full AGM reduction proof (#129 + issues #106–#116; core #88 already merged), which stays sorry'd behind the bound.

technique · AGM monomial analysis

A direct AGM argument parallel to Lemma 5.4, with no ufcmva term — 5.11 does not route through Theorem 5.1, and its Sign oracle is the server side of issuance only, so it needs neither the μCMZ KVAC instance nor the credential track. Each Sign query carries an algebraic representation of Cj (Eq 17); the associated polynomials cj (Eq 18) and forgery polynomials φi (Eq 22) live on the merged AGMPolynomial variable ring. Coefficient bookkeeping plus a pigeonhole over the q+1 distinct forged messages forces some φi ≠ 0, and three claims turn a surviving monomial into an assumption break: uι → DL (5.12), η → DL (5.13), x-monomials → 2-DL (5.14); the n > 1 collision case adds the gap-DL argument of Lemma 5.5 (shared with #80). Open modeling points, mirroring 5.1's: the AGM ↔ plain-game bridge (the #81 pattern) and whether issuance carries πis — Figure 9 keeps it, but the §5.6 proof answers Sign with (Uj, Vj) alone and the bound has no zk term, so the formalization targets the πis-less core and records the delta. Constants are provisional pending the degree audit (the paper's total-degree claim for φi fails at q = 1, and the 1/p terms share the documented 3/p Schwartz–Zippel pattern).
